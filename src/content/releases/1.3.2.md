A small update for Apple silicon Macs.

### Privacy

- Usage analytics are gone. The app sends no usage data, so its Settings
  switch and first-run notice are removed too.

### Markdown

- Wrapped paragraphs reflow at any zoom instead of breaking where the file's
  lines break.

### Security

- Equations in documents are cleaned before they are displayed.
- Downloading remote pictures checks the address actually connected to, not
  just the one requested.
