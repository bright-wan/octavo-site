The first release from this site, for Apple silicon Macs. It is signed and
notarized by Apple and updates itself from now on. Windows and Linux follow.

### Docs

- Footnotes and endnotes are edited in place at the bottom of the page.
- Text boxes are editable, and share one undo history with the body.
- Headers and footers save as you type, with their own undo.
- Undoing the deletion of commented text brings the comment back.
- Tracked changes and comments on note references and pictures are kept.
- Table of contents page numbers stay current.
- Tables keep the file's column widths.
- Pictures in older formats (metafile, TIFF) and symbol characters display.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean lines break by the document's language.
- Nested tables split correctly across pages.
- Documents open faster, and editing large documents is quicker.

### Sheets and Slides

- Google-style toolbars under the menus, like Docs.
- Sheets no longer loses keyboard focus while a file opens.

### Security

- Tightened how embedded HTML in Word files is converted.
- The app no longer grants extra privileges to local `file://` pages.
