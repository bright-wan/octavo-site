An update for Apple silicon Macs.

### PDF export

- PDFs exported on macOS 26 keep their Chinese text. Documents, slides,
  spreadsheets and Markdown used to print Chinese as blank space.

### Documents

- Tracked changes cover paragraph breaks: pressing Enter or joining two
  paragraphs while suggesting is a suggestion, saved as Word's paragraph-mark
  revisions.
- Deleting a selection that spans paragraphs, tables or pictures is tracked as
  one change.
- Headers and footers edit in place: the body moves down as you type, and
  page numbers, a different first page and header options are in reach.
- Comment cards have hover controls, replies, editing and reactions.
- Typing in long documents with many tables is much faster.
- Tables with merged cells keep their columns and row heights.
- Stray private-use characters from some exported documents no longer show
  as boxes.

### All editors

- When another program changes an open file on disk, Octavo asks whether to
  reload it.

### Home

- Open is the first card on the home screen. Missing files can be cleared
  from the list, and a document can be starred from its tab.
